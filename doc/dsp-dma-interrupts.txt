DSP56371 DMA interrupt events and native service

Primary references: DSP56300FM Rev5 table10-5 (DIE), table10-10 (DTD), and
section2.3.2; DSP56371UM Rev2.1 figures4-1/4-2 and tables4-4/4-5/4-6.
URLs, sizes and hashes are retained in hardware/sources.json; PDFs remain local.

IPRC bits12..23 contain six independent two-bit DMA IPL fields. Encoding0
disables; 1/2/3 select IPL0/1/2. Relative vectors18/1a/1c/1e/20/22 identify
DMA0..5. Within equal IPL, IRQA precedes DMA0..5, which precede SHI and TIM.
The original initializer's4006 selects IRQA edge IPL1 and DMA1 IPL0; it is
accepted through modeled fields rather than an ignored initialization write.

DIE in DCR bit22 enables end-of-block interrupts. An actual final destination
store and counter reload latch the request. Explicit DE clear also requests
service, after a captured word finishes its destination phase. A source read,
denied grant or failed destination cannot assert completion. The six independent
latches are not derived from reset DTD=1 or the repeating-mode DTD lifetime,
which remains unqualified. Repeated completions coalesce in the pending latch.

Native vector service acknowledges the selected channel after both vector words
are fetched, clearing its captured request without clearing DTD or other channels.
DIE gates delivery;
clearing it does not erase an already captured request. Enabling DIE over an
uncaptured old done status does not invent an end-of-block event. Reset clears
the latches. Trusted machine snapshots retain both DMA phase and IRQ state.
The conservative admitted/reference-vector source-hold checks also apply to
DMA; a withdrawn/changed source before acknowledgement requires coordinated
recovery rather than a fabricated successful service.

Fixed tests cover every field/CPU mask, all channel priority pairs, IRQA/DMA/
SHI/TIM tie order, all six channels in modes1/2/3/5 with DIE on/off, one- and
two-word block boundaries/counter reload, destination
wait/failure, explicit stop before/after a captured word, live DIE changes,
reset/copy, repeated events, and actual native fast/long vectors with real RTI.
Joint snapshots retain held data and pending requests; missing vector words and
suspended acknowledgement retain strict failures and recover through snapshots.

This extends the explicit standalone DMA reference phases, not automatic
native core/DMA bus arbitration. DCON/other transfer modes, missing inputs,
reserved accesses, unknown repeating DTD and unsupported physical timing still
fail. A successful priority write does not establish original initialization,
full MCU/DSP boot, live USB-MIDI, audio or hardware equivalence. No fuzzing or
physical traffic is run by these fixed regressions.
