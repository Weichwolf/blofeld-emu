Documented passive DSP GPIO bias

DSP56371UM Rev.2.1 tables2-8..2-11 specify internal pull-downs on PC0..11,
PE0..11, PD0..1 and PF0..10. The primary download identity is in
hardware/sources.json. Local GPIO resolution applies that bias only to pins
explicitly declared floating and not owned by output/peripheral drivers.
An unobserved board connection or active unknown driver remains unknown.
The bias does not initialize an unknown output latch or qualify a peripheral.
Externally resolved pad networks retain their own explicit driver contracts.

  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-dsp-gpio-test blofeld-dsp-gpio-pull-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -R '^dsp_gpio(_pull)?$' --output-on-failure

The independent all-pin regression checks seven observations on every one of
the37 implemented pins: unknown connection, floating input low, driven high,
active unknown, unknown output provenance, and known high/low outputs. The
unchanged test object reports25 floating-input failures against the former
C-only implementation. Directed controls retain their stricter expectations.
The older other-port floating-input refusals contradicted the documented bias;
their pad/input expectations now require the specified low level.

This fixes a local GPIO property. Actual board routing, Port-F BIU timing,
reserved-one writes, the original broad memory clear and normal instrument
startup retain their separate unresolved contracts.
