Original MIDI during the foreground DMA wait

original_dsp_idle_midi retains ten original foreground blocks in eight pitch,
rounding and filter-route contexts. Between each body and the next paused-core
serial phase, raw bytes enter only the primary ring and producer pointer. Two
real idle polls per chunk execute the unchanged parser and equal-half gate.
Running status, fragmented CC7, another channel, coalesced targets and a 48-byte
burst cross the 64-word input ring. Empty polls must retain parser staging.

Independent MIDI/63:64 smoothing/voice/master equations check 192 idle polls,
80 body blocks, 2560 DMA words and 1280 stereo frames. The target changes while
idle; current gain, scheduler counters, seed, audio RAM, enabled DMA and serial
state remain unchanged until their actual next body/transfer. Original program
words and native caller frames are checked. Nine corrupt trace controls reject.

original_dsp_ring_midi_audio submits all eight ten-block contexts directly to
eight source-owned AudioRuntime instances: all1280 independently predicted S32LE
stereo frames must match an isolated no-rewind monitor in order under ASan/UBSan
and separately TSan. Each client submits actual pin-decoded frames per half,
without reading a trace or capture. All192 idle polls,72 fixed trace corruptions,
eight monitor corruptions and eight rejected foreign source claims are checked.

Reports/traces are under Testing/original-dsp-idle-midi.* and
Testing/original-dsp-ring-midi-audio/. Missing firmware/tools skip, changed
identities or native diagnostics fail. Manufacturer assets remain local.

Scope: untimed native reference admissions alternate with paused-core HCKT/DMA
phases. Effects Off, held entry, readable initial RAM and non-due part clock are
explicit fixture inputs. Playback starts at EOF below the host prebuffer. This
does not establish physical arbitration/clocks, realtime production, full boot,
live host MIDI, NoteOn allocation or real-device equivalence. No fuzzing runs.
