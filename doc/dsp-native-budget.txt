OwnDspAutomaticTiming::native_cycle_budget is selected explicitly in C++, or with
dsp-execution native-cycles in a format2 text config. reference-effects retains
its admitted-effect ordering. Both text choices are required explicitly; complete
normal device profiles remain unqualified. Instruction semantics and original
firmware remain unchanged. CLI qualification from actual DSP reset is recorded
separately in reference-native-cycle-cli-reference.json.

Each admitted period advances DMA/F2 and at most the first external CPU effect.
Consecutive CPU-only address/MOVE/ALU/unit/PCU publications share that period.
The actual retirement ticket waits until the core's computed native base budget
has elapsed. A conflicting DMA destination holds native work; an independent
DMA transfer does not. Timers and actual elapsed clocks continue during waits.
No early retirement, skipped instruction, substituted bus result or clock scaling
is used. After genuine retirement the synchronized frontend may issue its next
F1. REP and vector latches retain their native ownership and actual read rules.

Joint snapshots retain paid periods and pending retirement. Mixed manual/reference
timing refuses during an active budget. Reset discards the old continuation.
Required unknown data keeps its original address/PC fault; interrupt acknowledgement
still follows both real vector reads, including a refused second word.

Authored actual-reset cases check sequential NOP1, REP setup5,23 repeated NOPs23,
native GPIO transitions, operand failures, padding/restore and timing refusals.
DMA cases compare independent Y RAM with a conflicting P-bank destination,
actual sampled data, a held destination and both restored suffixes. IRQ cases
assert during a two-period MOVE, then verify genuine vector reads, acknowledgement,
fast return and missing-word refusal. Reset during padding restarts the real ROM.
Both sanitizer profiles also pass affected fetch, timeline and ownership regressions.

Limits: base units exclude physical pipeline, arithmetic/AGU interlocks, IRQ-entry
overhead and bus waits. A CPU-only AGU prefix before a later operand can add a
conservative period in this model. Initial F1/F2 filling and normal instruction
phase placement are not calibrated; steady-cadence fixtures explicitly prefill
known words. Correct original overlay transfer, full startup, normal MIDI/audio
and both complete instrument profiles remain unproven by these component tests.
