unknown costs/dependencies stop planning. Control and DO endpoints terminate the
plan. REP retains the real Core latch. Native execution remains enabled.

staged retirement; changing a host-supplied word cannot replace that offer.

Own normal-NXP timing windows accompany real CPU/register/peripheral assertions,
checkpoint/mutation, REP/DO, native-control and ownership controls. This component
is not yet bound to the Board timeline/PLL/DMA clock or original IRQ entry.
