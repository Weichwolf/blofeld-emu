Internal TFR may accompany A0/A1/A2/B0/B1/B2 -> X/Y:(Rn),
(Rn)+, (Rn)-, (Rn)+Nn or (Rn)-Nn. FM13-118/122/178 and Tables12-13/14
define the independent move/source encodings. A0/A1/B0/B1 transfer raw24 bits;
A2/B2 sign-extend their8 bits. These sources bypass full-accumulator limiting
and scaling. TFR transfers the opposite accumulator or sign-extended
X0/X1/Y0/Y1 into full A/B, clearing its low part for a data-register source.
TFR preserves CCR and uses the old source when its destination also supplies
the parallel memory write.

Native AG1/AG2 owns R/N/M calculation and pointer publication; E1 performs
the actual old-part write and E2 retires TFR. FM3.5.2.1/A.2.3 source interlocks
use only the MOVE source: an adjacent writer of that accumulator adds one
period. ALU-only opposite-accumulator dependencies retain existing forwarding.
The reference policy holds F1/F2 and younger stages during that period while
the older E2 retires. This is not an observed physical fetch trace.

Per sanitizer profile,20080 own windows check complete CPU/CCR/stack/counters,
exact elapsed periods,F2/data footprint,old-value aliases and untouched cells.
They cover all six parts,five TFR sources,both destinations/spaces,three scaling
modes,five addressing modes,all eight Rn and256-word modulo wrap. Ten preceding
partial/full/ADD writers use0/1/2 gaps. Fifty cuts restore and clone into fresh
owners; backing program replacement after Decode cannot change captured words.
Thirty classification guards retain unsupported neighboring forms and REP.

This predicate does not widen standalone partial stores or REP bodies.
Pre-update/extended addressing,other parallel ALU forms and reserved scaling
retain separate refusals. Automatic SC=1 and physical timing remain unqualified.
These are instruction-model checks,not complete original boot,MIDI/audio,
update/recovery or hardware equivalence.
