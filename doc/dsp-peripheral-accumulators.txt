Full-accumulator peripheral loads

FM12.2.1/13-134..136 MOVEP pp/qq,A/B sign-extends the actual24-bit peripheral
word into the extension/MSP and clears the low24bits. CCR,scaling and arithmetic
saturation modes do not alter this load. The existing owned AG1 response is
sampled once; native E1 publishes all three actual completed accumulator fields.
Native flags/PC/counters retire in E2. Bare staged callers still fail before
I/O without the actual automatic AG1 response owner. No host result is supplied.

An adjacent A1/B1 bus consumer interlocks one period; a NOP separates it, and
the opposite accumulator stays independent. A preceding CLR retains its real
CCR result while the newer MOVEP replaces the selected accumulator. Destructive
HRX reads consume two real SPI-supplied words at their own AG1 periods; replay
restores FIFO consumption together with the native/pipeline state. Unknown or
partially known full words fail with address/operation/PC/OMR before publication.
