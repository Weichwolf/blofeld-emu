Standalone A1/B1 -> X0/X1/Y0/Y1/R0..7 copies all24 bits without running the
full-accumulator shifter/limiter. SR and the source accumulator remain intact,
including overflowing extensions and scaling modes0..3. Full A/B transfers keep
their separate scaling/limiting rules. Other partial sources remain excluded.
Qualified parallel TFR/ADD forms are documented in dsp-parallel-middle-transfers.txt.

FM3.5.1/3.5.2.1/A.2.3 and complete NXP reference windows support one extra period
when the preceding one-word producer writes any part of the same accumulator.
Foreign accumulators or an intervening instruction do not interlock. Two-word
writers already supply separation. Raw A1/B1 -> Y1 supplies literal DOR counts;
actual calls may overwrite A1 and run an inner loop while preserving that Y1.
See dsp-raw-middle-reference.json for exact reference identities/limits and the
isolated assembler transport-fault review. Vendor pipeline warnings are retained;
project compiler/tidy checks remain strict.

118 own reset-preamble/RAM streams check complete CPU,table counters and all16
stack cells every period. Exact destination lanes publish at native E1; PC and
instruction-table counters retire at E2.692 cuts across18 raw/interlocked/
multiword/count/call cases restore twice per sanitizer profile. Initial seeds,
scaling bits and source extensions have independent literal checks.

Subsequent SR-read timing keeps its separate uncertainty/refusals documented in
dsp-status-pipeline.txt. Arithmetic/memory producers,other partial consumers,
unqualified parallel forms,original near-end calls and full original reset/MIDI/audio/storage/
update/recovery/realtime/physical acceptance remain open. Reserved Y:002000 is
unchanged; these own fixtures do not replace connected original-firmware boot.
