Original MIDI release through foreground, retirement and audio

original_dsp_release_ring executes ten original foreground bodies in eight
pitch/rounding/filter-route contexts. The original list initializer supplies the
unused list tail before voice setup. After body2, actual Note Off or zero-velocity
Note On bytes enter the primary ring during the equal-half wait.
The original parser gathers the held entry using its aliased Y coefficient
scratch and replaces the four envelope callbacks. No host updates the slot,
gain, list, phase, DMA or CPU continuation after rendering begins.

Original factory releases remain0/0/64/64. The first two envelopes truncate
to0, select retirement on the next call, then become idle. Body5 removes the
entry through the original collector/owner helper and retains the collection
flag. Body6 clears it after the empty scan. Four subsequent empty-list bodies
continue original mix/master/scheduler and continuously enabled DMA/ESAI output.
The master filter's tail is predicted; silence is not substituted for it.

Independent integer models check48 complete voice returns,80 foreground bodies,
32 empty bodies, eight retirements and1280 stereo frames. Full envelope/cache
state, native call counts, source/list/flags, scheduler endpoints, DMA addresses
and delayed serial halves are checked. Empty bodies must preserve the entire
retired slot. Ten corrupted trace controls reject. Original program words and
the automatic-DMA timing refusal remain checked.

original_dsp_ring_release_audio submits one actual decoded ten-body stream to
AudioRuntime per half;160 independently predicted S32LE frames must match a
private no-rewind monitor under ASan/UBSan and separately TSan. No trace or
completed-stream capture supplies the native client.

Reports/traces: Testing/original-dsp-release-ring.* and
Testing/original-dsp-ring-release-audio/. Missing firmware/tools skip; changed
identities or native diagnostics fail. Manufacturer bytes remain local.

Scope: declared held entry/readable RAM, zero owners and Effects Off. Untimed
reference admissions alternate with paused-core serial/grant phases; playback
starts at EOF below the host prebuffer. Positive allocation, complete boot,
physical timing, realtime, live host MIDI and hardware equivalence remain open.
No fuzzing or physical traffic runs.
