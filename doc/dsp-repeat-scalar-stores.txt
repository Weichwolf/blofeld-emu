Repeated full-accumulator scalar stores

  ctest --test-dir build/strict -R '^dsp_repeat_scalar(_guard)?$' --output-on-failure

DSP56300FM Rev5 13-160/161 defines positive REP count ownership, one body
fetch and saved LC restoration. Scalar source/flag rules: dsp-scalar-stores.txt.

The fetch and Decode guards share the same qualified-body predicate: NOP,
existing full A->L:(R4)+, and standalone full A/B->X/Y stores in modes0..4.
Native execution owns all LC, address, data and retirement effects. The body
retains its actual F2 receipt even if the own test program changes afterward.

Independent checks cover 2160 streams: A/B, X/Y, immediate/X0 counts1/3/17,
three signed56 values, three scales, R0/R7 and five linear address modes.
They check every complete CPU state, nominal setup5/body1, exact stores,
address updates, unchanged gaps/canaries and saved LC. Additional checks cover
128 snapshot cuts with two replays, eight latch mutations, ten unsupported
body forms and four reserved-scale cases with two refusal replays each.

An own zero-source stream clears 6848 X words from0936 through23f5. These are
stored workspace dimensions, not original reset/initializer execution. Other
body forms, zero/other count sources, active DO/cache and automatic IRQ remain
unqualified. Authentic ROM identity, reserved Y:002000 and normal original
instrument operation remain open; no hardware timing/equivalence claim.
