The private firmware, MID and all DSP records are hash-checked before execution.
dsp-sample-reference.json selects the unchanged sampler program and coefficient
records. An own ROM caller supplies explicit unscaled arithmetic, slot/FM inputs,
linear coefficient addressing and an aligned 512-word ring. The original callback
updates phase, reads the ring with modulo512 and jumps through its real continuation.

The 37 program words contain 36 instruction starts and EIGHT products: one MPY
and seven MACs, with eight coefficient and eight sample reads. Earlier source-side
prose claiming nine taps contradicts those instructions. Firmware and interpreter instructions remain unchanged.

The independent Python model checks signed phase/FM arithmetic, raw A1 storage,
folded coefficient rows/stride and the truncated full-accumulator dot product.
144384 complete calls cover all32768 fractions in four banks,13056 logical PCM
windows from all136 official sample headers, and256 signed/zero/FM corner cases.
Actual native accesses must match every coefficient address and PCM word. Output
and exposed sample agree; slot/output guards, continuation, cursor updates and
M5-to-M1 restoration are checked. The ninth supplied word is never read.

Independent row L1 maxima are2767021,2132450,2097150,2097150. Every bound is below
8388608, so these original tables cannot clip the eight-tap result for signed24
samples in this unscaled mode. FM intermediate limiting is exercised separately.

WPC/Spectre identities and decoded logical PCM follow sample-library.txt. Each
declared PCM word is high-aligned from signed16; no WPC-to-physical-NAND or native
header conversion is inferred. Rate/bank preparation, requests/refills, sample
installation, continuous voices, board scheduling, physical audio and full boot
remain unqualified. Reserved Y:002000 and other unknown accesses remain strict.
