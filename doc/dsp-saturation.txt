DSP arithmetic saturation and defined host arithmetic

DSP56300FM Rev.5 sections 3.1.5, 3.2.2/3 and the instruction tables define
arithmetic saturation (SR.SM, bit20). Table3-1 selects saturation from EXT7,
EXT0 and MSP23 of the 56-bit result after rounding. The other extension bits
do not participate. Positive/negative rails are 007fffffffffff/ff800000000000;
rounded positive saturation is 007fffff000000, independent of scaling mode.
V reports saturation, L is sticky, and E/U/N/Z describe the limited result.
Transfer saturation on the data buses is a separate operation.

The native reference patch applies this rule before result flags are cached.
Single-bit ALU ASL/ASR and NORM participate; counted ASL/ASR use the BFU and
remain exempt even for count1. TFR/Tcc, mixed/unsigned MPY/MAC/DMAC and other
BFU operations also retain their documented exceptions. Products, accumulation
and rounding use unpadded signed host integers with enough headroom for the
mathematical result. This removes a legal MAC signed-host overflow. Rounding
precedes saturation atomically for MPYR/MACR and immediate/power forms.

Deterministic checks (no fuzz or physical-device traffic)
  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-dsp-saturation-test blofeld-dsp-corpus-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -R '^dsp_(saturation_.*|hardware_corpus)$' --output-on-failure

Seven groups contain 81687 fixed cases: eight named regressions, 24330 arithmetic,
11448 multiply, 24948 accumulate, 6912 rounding, 14040 exception/transfer cases,
and one preserved legal MAC host-overflow input. Cases cross A/B, SM off/on,
three scaling modes and CCR seeds, with RM off/on for rounded instructions.
The first six groups cover both signed endpoints, all table3-1 selector rows,
unused extension bits, carry/borrow, sticky flags, rounding ties and signedness.
Power coefficients use the explicitly documented n=1,8,22 encodings.

An independent oracle uses signed integer range/division/remainders and a
literal eight-row saturation table. Real native setup instructions supply all
operands and status. Checks include the entire CPU state, stack, instruction
length and retirement. C after ADDL/SUBL shift overflow has no asserted silicon
oracle under the manual's qualification; all other fields remain checked.
Handwritten documented product and counted-shift words avoid ambiguous or
unsupported helper-assembler aliases. No manufacturer program bytes are added.

Before the correction, all seven groups failed, including UBSan for the legal
MAC input D=7fffffffffffff, X0=Y0=7fffff. The private corrected native candidate
passes every case. All 17 unchanged b9 MCPX capture cases match 748 observed
register values, alongside the four previously adopted banks. Memory and stack
are unobserved in b9 and are not inferred as zero (dsp-validation.txt). These are selected
MCPX observations, not a Blofeld/DSP56371 measurement or a full-ISA proof.

Sixteen-bit arithmetic SA, SM-specific compare/test behavior, pipeline timing,
reserved scaling and other derivatives remain unqualified. The independent
SA=0 vectors do not establish the virtual 40-bit SA arithmetic paths. Full
original DSP boot still stops on reserved Y:002000; original board assets,
MIDI/audio and hardware validation remain separate outstanding requirements.
