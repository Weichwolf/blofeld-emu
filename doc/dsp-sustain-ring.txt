Original sustain through foreground, retirement and host audio

Eight original streams retain the equal-half DMA gate. CC64=1/127 arrives
after body 0; Note Off/zero-velocity Note On arrives after body 2 and remains
latched without changing envelope callbacks. After body 4, fragmented CC64=0
releases the entry only when its last byte arrives. No host supplies callbacks,
slot/list/phase changes, decoded frames or a manufactured return.

Factory release controls stay 0/0/64/64. Original envelopes retire the first
two callbacks in body 7; the real collector removes the held entry. Four empty
bodies retain the master-filter tail and continuously enabled DMA/ESAI output.
Independent models check 64 full voice returns, 96 foreground bodies, 32 empty
bodies, eight retirements and 1536 stereo frames. Twelve corrupted traces reject.
Sustain scalar/flag/latches, idle parser state, full envelope/cache histories,
source/list/flags, scheduler endpoints and serial/DMA phases are checked.
The model retains the original post-scan parser status 0x70 from shared scratch;
it does not repair that context or qualify later running-status traffic.

One direct 192-frame stream reaches AudioRuntime per decoded half and must match
a private no-rewind monitor. Both sanitizers use the actual native source;
no trace or completed-stream capture supplies the client.
