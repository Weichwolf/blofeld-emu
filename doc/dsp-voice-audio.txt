The unchanged original voice/copy/mix/master and actual DMA/ESAI path from
dsp-voice-pcm.txt produces 32 streams of 128 complete SerialPcmFrame objects.
A bounded offline collector retains the actual objects after each completed
peripheral replay. Delivery sees only the final branch, once. No samples or
timestamps are reconstructed from the trace or synthesized by the host.
The collector stays outside modeled checkpoints and is immutable during playback.

After all rendering finishes, each source gets a fresh AudioRuntime worker.
It accepts all 128 frames and closes/drains the actual native service stream.
The short closed source starts without padding; accepted/written counts must
both equal 128. Existing worker rules reject underruns before complete EOF.
No source identity, clock or prior host stream is reused across native resets.

The private PulseAudio null sink uses norewinds=1, stereo S32LE and 48000 Hz.
The declared timebase is 24576000 ticks/s: eight ticks/bit, 64 bits/frame.
This assigns a laboratory playback rate to the existing edge ordinals;
it establishes neither physical Blofeld frequency nor realtime rendering.
The test connects only its private service/monitor; the user's service, default
output, routes and hardware remain untouched. Debian tools: pulseaudio-utils,
pulseaudio. Missing tools or private firmware skip explicitly.

Archive/MID and all 127 ordered record identities are checked first. Independent
voice/mix/master equations check 8192 DMA words and 4096 frames before playback.
Those equations also produce every expected native S32 container. The monitor
must contain all
32 complete streams in order, with only zero-valued service gaps around them;
truncated, missing, duplicated, stale or additional nonzero samples fail.
Traces, monitor PCM, counts and exact offsets stay under build/Testing/.

ASan/UBSan and TSan run separately, including instrumented native client/codecs.
No new concurrency mechanism, sanitizer suppression or successful device stub
is introduced. Existing parser, boot, allocator and DMA qualification faults
remain. Whole boot, live MIDI allocation, continuous realtime production,
PipeWire/device moves/rewinds and physical device equivalence remain open.
