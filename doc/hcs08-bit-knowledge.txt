HCS08 BIT and GPIO input knowledge

Native BIT immediate preserves A/HX/SP and executes its original SLEIGH effects.
Only the tested bits determine V/N/Z knowledge. Bits outside the immediate mask
are known zero in the temporary AND result; they do not become known in A.
A known selected one establishes nonzero even if other selected bits are unknown.
A dependent BEQ/BNE refuses if Z is still unknown. BNE, like BEQ, preserves
partial A/HX rather than requiring unrelated register bits to be known.

Hcs08Gpio::read_known_byte retains the resolved knowledge of implemented inputs.
Outputs read their actual latches; documented non-pin fields read known zero.
Each input set is sampled once. Detached sources, invalid observations and
opposed drivers still fail with address/PC/context. Ordinary full-byte GPIO
reads remain strict. Hcs08BoardMemory routes native masked LDA through this API;
other consumers keep their existing permissions and knownness requirements.
No voltage, board connection, frequency, ready status or success is supplied.

The own native BIT regression enumerates all6561 canonical partial byte states
and all256 immediates:1679616 complete states. Independent enumeration of every
concrete candidate determines the expected flags. Directed LDA/BIT/BEQ/BNE paths,
trusted replay, unknown deciding flags and the existing3/2/3-period retirement
contract supplement this matrix; no physical bus phase is inferred.
The GPIO gate checks15360 port/direction/knowledge/level cases against finite
physical-byte candidates, plus strict reads, conflicts and detached copies.

The original_hcs08_board_memory gate retains its prior three reset cases.
A fourth original reset observes only PTED3 high; other input pins remain
unknown. It must reach the identical6197-retirement timer boundary with the
same complete native state, all1024 RAM bytes and masks, ICG/ADC/TPM/COP/SPI/
FLASH state, and unchanged supplied NVM. A fifth executes original LDA/BIT with
all input pins unresolved and requires the real BEQ atE052 to reject unknown Z
after6178 instructions. Unchanged firmware still owns every decision.
The declared high pin/calibration and untimed candidate part are laboratory
inputs, not measured Blofeld wiring. Normal controller/full instrument startup,
authentic clock domains, idle FSTAT launch, original DSP audio/MIDI, persistence,
update/recovery/every power-loss phase and real hardware equivalence remain open.
