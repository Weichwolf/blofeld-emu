Pinned HCS08RMV1 Rev.2 printed250: DIV divides H:A by X, returning quotient A
and remainder H; X and CCR V/H/I/N remain unchanged. C reports zero divisor or
quotient>255. On either error, A and register H are indeterminate; therefore Z
cannot be known. CCR.H is a separate preserved flag, not register H.

The local SLEIGH patch guards zero division before its host arithmetic. Its
representatives are masked by native register/CCR knowledge at retirement;
zero representatives do not establish hardware zero results. Successful DIV
defines A/H and C/Z. Errors leave only X known, retain unrelated flag knowledge,
define C=1 and clear Z knowledge. All H/A/X operand bits must be known before
execution. Guest branches can consume known C; unknown A/H/Z cannot silently
become known stores, stack bytes or TPA results. Guest LDA/CLRH restore knowledge
through actual instructions. CPU execution/observer/reservation code is unchanged.

Independent wide division/remainder expectations cover22449 finite boundary
cases: every divisor, zero/max quotient, remainder boundaries, quotient256 and
maximum dividend, with stale/preserved flag seeds. Nine partial-CCR cases,
24 missing operand bits, three invalid consumers and four fresh post-error
continuations check knowledge. Six-period DIV plus native BCS/LDA/CLRH/BGND
finishes in17 reference periods; four fresh joint CPU/RAM/BDC/clock suffixes
start two periods into DIV, with four remaining. Timing stays the explicit
instruction-retirement reference; bus phases and silicon measurements remain open.

This corrects CPU semantics needed before original calibration-gain acquisition.
It does not establish that acquisition, full boot, MIDI/audio or device/update
recovery equivalence. Original ICG/FSTAT/DSP reserved-memory frontiers remain
strict. No fuzz or physical traffic. Primary identities: hcs08-reference.json.
