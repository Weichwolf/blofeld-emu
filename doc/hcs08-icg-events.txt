ICG fault-event latches (candidate GT8A)

Primary: hcs08-reference.json, GT9.3.2/9.3.3/9.4.8/9.4.9.
Hcs08IcgEvents owns LOLS/LOCS/ICGIF from established detector events.
Its known mask 0x15 excludes clock/mode/LOCK/DCOS bits. A status read arms
interrupt acknowledgement; writing ICGIF=1 clears interrupt-routed causes.
A new IRQ invalidates the earlier read, including a repeated latched source.
Reset-routed causes persist until explicit reset; reset requests take priority.
Changing routing for an already latched source remains a visible unsupported
transaction; unrelated routing changes preserve its read history.

Copies retain latch, routing and read history without callbacks or peers.
The full register owner must validate all clock/status bits before committing
the latch read. This component neither detects clock loss nor generates a
frequency, LOCK/DCOS, mode transition, CPU reset or complete ICGS1 response.
The existing strict ICG owner and normal-reset frontier remain unchanged.

  ctest --test-dir build/strict -R '^hcs08_icg_events$' --output-on-failure

The fixed byte matrix, event races and copies cover 393228 cases, 1536 fresh
suffixes and 4 refusal controls per sanitizer. No native firmware, full clock,
board timing, audio callback or physical-device equivalence is established.
