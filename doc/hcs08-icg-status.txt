HCS08 candidate observed ICG status

Hcs08IcgStatus composes independently supplied synchronized CLKST/REFST/LOCK/
ERCS/DCOS observations with Hcs08IcgEvents fault/read-ack ownership. Raw CLKST
is a two-bit status field, independent of software CLKS requests. SCM/FBE
prove LOCK clear; contradictory observations reject before mutation.

read_known retains unknown bits and performs the genuine ICGS1 read/ack-arm
side effect. Ordinary byte reads reject incomplete facts before admission.
ICGS2 bits7..1 read zero. Writes ignore read-only data; a masked ICGS1 write
requires known ICGIF. Another interrupt invalidates an older read-clear pair.
Copies retain observations and armed sequence; reset discards live facts.

No oscillator, status detector, elapsed-time synchronization, CPU load/store
knownness consumer or board binding is supplied. Unknown bits are never ready
or stable defaults. Candidate part/mask and complete original boot remain
unqualified. Primary:hcs08-reference.json GT Rev1 sections9.3.3/9.3.4.

After integration:build blofeld-hcs08-icg-status-test;run CTest hcs08_icg_status
under each sanitizer separately. Per mode:351 accepted observed points,
54 impossible LOCK points and3072 masked fault/read/ack cases plus strict
context/reset checks. No fuzz or physical traffic.
