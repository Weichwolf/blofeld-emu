Family RM Rev.2 printed87,240,286 agree: CLI/TAP changing I1->0 protects the
following instruction. Polling sources, offered periods, debugger holds and
partial instruction budgets cannot consume that guarantee. A second CLI with
I already0 does not extend it. Unknown old/restored I never selects a guessed
transition. Unresolved eligibility fails with vector/PC/CCR knowledge.

RTI has conflicting descriptions in the same pinned primary: p87 says its
I-clear delay expires before RTI finishes; RTI detail p275 guarantees a returned
instruction. The GT16A summary does not resolve this. The default RTI contract
is unqualified: a restored unmasked pending source faults before arbitration
or another frame byte. With no source, an actual completed following instruction
resolves the difference. Restored I still independently masks or remains unknown.

Explicit reference_immediate/reference_next_instruction contracts permit the
two source-described experiments. They are conditional reference models, not
hardware qualification. Checkpoints retain delay and immutable RTI contract;
a receiving CPU with a different contract rejects restoration before mutation.
Debugger register replacement clears execution history. BDC GO instead resumes
history and clears only its consumed background request; it cannot discard
another owner's instruction reservation or retained IRQ queue.

Independent transition/flag/native-frame cases, actual CLI/TAP/RTI, unknown
masks, repeated queries, real instruction/IRQ reservations, held clocks, pre/
mid/post budgets, observer refusal and fresh graph continuations qualify the
state. Default-conflict, both RTI reference choices and contract mismatch have
separate expectations. Native data failure retains delay and ownership until
coordinated recovery. No physical bus/prefetch/late-sampling or full boot/normal
IRQ scheduler claim follows. No fuzz or physical traffic.

Primary URL/size/hash: hcs08-reference.json. Peer WIP was reviewed from a frozen
read-only copy; its source/index are unchanged. Queue consumption and combined
CPU/ADC/SPI edge scheduling remain separate requirements.

The existing original SPI IRQ gate selects reference_immediate explicitly. Its
unchanged trace tests the declared untimed schedule, not physical RTI latency.
