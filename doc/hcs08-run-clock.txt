Hcs08RunClock consumes individual target bus periods independently of host
status reads. The caller must select reference_instruction_retirement; an
unqualified contract rejects. Family RM Rev.2 Table6-13 (printed130/131),
Table6-12 and instruction details supply all ordinary unstretched costs:
251 primary forms plus47 page2 forms. Primary9E dispatches to page2; therefore
252 first-opcode classifications accept. Undefined8D/AC and STOP8E/WAIT8F refuse.
STOP/WAIT require indefinite hold/wake owners; their minimum2 periods do not
establish retirement. BGND uses the existing separate BDC held-state owner.

The decoder groups addressing costs and names special forms explicitly.
Notable exceptions: MUL5, DIV6, RTI9, SWI11; CPHX direct5 and page2 SP1 CPHX6;
indexed STA/STX without displacement2; DBNZ SP1=8; four-byte SP2/LDHX16 fetches.
An independent literal matrix checks all256 primary and256 secondary values,
every missing instruction byte, every fetch-wrap boundary and two CCR contexts.
The previous50 first-opcode/eight secondary timing tuples remain explicit checks.
No opcode-only cost can hide an unknown operand or perform an architectural read.

Timing metadata does not establish native semantic support: BIH/BIL still need
a qualified IRQ-pin intrinsic, and STOP/WAIT remain unsupported. Native DAA
is qualified separately in hcs08-decimal.txt; this is not bus-phase evidence.
Native errors remain visible. No fixed-period entry claims hardware bus/prefetch,
queue consumption or board-clock fidelity.
Shared CPU/ADC reference-clock evidence: hcs08-adc.txt. Original calibrated
pitch/IRQ/SPI/SHI evidence remains instruction-driven: hcs08-pitch-event.txt.
Shared-clock calibrated producer/overlapping ADC evidence: hcs08-pitch-clock.txt.

BGND's indefinite held interval is separate from its five execution periods.
Undefined/held opcode forms, unknown operands, wrapped fetches, mid-instruction
code/register changes and unqualified BDC halt arbitration fail visibly. Native
architectural effects commit at the end of the documented budget, including
actual CPU stores and BGND admission through the BDC owner. Failed instructions
retain elapsed periods without incrementing retired instructions. Held BDC time
never fetches or executes target instructions. There is no host ready result.

The independent unit checks every period of an own six-instruction program,
literal PC/HX/SP/A/CCR/store boundaries, all256 opcode classifications, held
intervals, native access failure/quarantine and two fresh CPU/memory/BDC/clock
restores from a partially elapsed LDA. TXS uses the complete H:X minus one;
LDX immediate retains H. A second own ten-instruction path checks MUL17*19=0143,
H/C clearing, PSHA/CLRA/PULA, JSR/TAX/RTS, stack byte order and BGND against literal
register states at every one of33 periods. Two fresh joint continuations from
inside MUL retain its three remaining periods and complete access histories.
Checkpoints do not replace separate CPU/memory/BDC
restoration or clear failed native execution.
All256 secondary opcodes are classified independently; unknown prefix/operand
bytes and both prefix/full-fetch wraps refuse. Conditional branches have the
same documented cost for taken and untaken paths.

Instruction admission and late IRQ ownership are qualified separately in

instruction budget and finishes an accepted instruction before another source

This explicit reference contract does not model per-cycle operand/prefetch

or concurrent BDC arbitration. Its periods are not a calibrated board frequency.
These remain required before full normal controller execution and physical
update/recovery equivalence. No concurrency, realtime callback, physical traffic
or fuzzing is introduced. Primary URL/size/hash: hcs08-reference.json.
