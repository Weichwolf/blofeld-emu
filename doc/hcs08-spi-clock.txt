GT12.3/12.4.3 tables12-5/6 derive the active SPSCK divider from BUSCLK.
Hcs08SpiClock requires reference_half_period_and_final_edge explicitly:
begin_transfer promotes an actual queued byte; the first edge follows one
complete half-period and completion follows edge16. The board selects each
promotion boundary. Idle periods never start a queued byte automatically.
These are conditional phase choices, not observed silicon timing. GT12.4.4
bounds idle promotion within2 BUSCLK; it does not fix our exact first-edge or
SPRF phase. Physical phase qualification and automatic double-buffer launch
remain open. Sources/identities: hcs08-reference.json and hcs08-spi.txt.

Each advance consumes one BUSCLK. Only due edges sample MISO/shift MOSI.
Returned pins retain MOSI at the final sampling event before the peripheral
releases its unspecified idle data output. The board must publish that event
to the peer. No peer/callback is retained and no received byte is injected.
Status/data access may change flag acknowledgements and queue the next byte
without consuming time. Active baud/format/shifter ownership changes refuse
before another period. Actual SPE disable/reset aborts without completion.

Unknown MISO fails at its real sample boundary, retaining elapsed periods and
previous edges. Peripheral and clock require joint recovery. Opaque checkpoints
retain partial divider/transfer counters; restore first requires the separately
restored peripheral including pending bytes and acknowledgement history.
Clock reset requires a separately reset/disabled healthy peripheral.

2048 finite baud/format cases compare every period/edge/sample against literal
prescaler/divider tables and bit expectations. Paired DSP-SHI transfers use
384 BUSCLK/48 pin events for three bytes.385 pre/mid/post cuts and770 fresh
continuations after original destruction reproduce full clock/SPI state,
pin/byte history and peer outputs/received word. Directed tests check unknown
sampling, foreign owners, active divisor changes, reset and restore refusal.
No fuzz or physical traffic. This adds no normal board boot, CPU IRQ/prefetch
consumer, common CPU/ADC/SPI edge schedule, native DSP firmware or live audio.
