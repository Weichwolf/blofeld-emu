Future instrument programs

After establishing the original-firmware emulator as a reliable test target,
develop Blofeld programs that reproduce the instruments in reference MIDI
performances as accurately as possible, preferably without external samples.
Evaluate the available original synthesis mechanisms by their audible results.
The user's sound-design ideas and experiences are suggestions, not prescribed
methods, parameter choices or design constraints.

User-supplied MIDI performances and sample recordings remain local references.
Use them for repeatable musical input and later timbre/dynamics comparisons;
they are not permission to redistribute assets or substitute host synthesis.
This future sound-design objective does not qualify current boot, MIDI voice
allocation, realtime audio, update/recovery or physical hardware behavior.
