Original initial Keyboard sender, display helper and resident return

  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-original-keyboard-initial-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -j1 -R '^original_keyboard_initial$' --output-on-failure

Pinned local main1.25 and Keyboard Controller1.4 archives are required. Their
identities, full original records and packet words are checked before execution.
The original DSP packet decoder downloads Keyboard blocks0/1 into one owner,
then its final JMP enters the unchanged initial sender. Own linear/unscaled
packet callers, reference clocks and IRQ/pad routing are explicit preconditions.
No raw MIDI/USB reception or original outer dispatcher is supplied here.

The sender patches the original main-loop word at P:019a with a branch back two
words, independently assembled from own source. All other words of that complete
original record stay exact. Native REP clears64 scratch words. Forty-one actual
Y reads supply164 six-bit writes: length119, the complete MCU helper and two
alignment bytes. The following object-checksum word is not sent. Native XIRQ/RTI,
resident receive/copy and JSR supply119 exact bytes and the real service table.

Five actual helper indicator calls toggle Q7. The first releases the sender's
final acknowledgement wait; original pin cleanup and RTS return to an explicitly
declared own idle caller. No additional packet is supplied by that caller.
The helper calls actual resident clear/text/bitmap functions and returns. A
separate original-font column/rectangle calculation predicts every visible PBM
pixel and1559 writes/1070 reads per case. Four PBM/PNG captures remain local.

The resident loader then drains nine decoded bytes. Two are cached alignment
bytes; seven more require twelve physical sample instructions after the sender
has released its data drivers. Under the existing explicit logical Port-C
pull-down model those samples are zero. Source-word count remains41. Native bus
release, CLI and RTS complete with statusc0 and the original caller stack.
These bytes do not establish a checksum or programming-success indication.

Four order/phase contexts each checkpoint after actual helper RTS. Withdrawing
the fixture's pad observation fails the first real sample after two cached bytes
at MCU PCbad3/address0260. DDRQ=80 leaves seven inputs: unused Q6 must remain known
for that read too. Native retry/capture reject without state/time changes.
Two trusted joint restorations per context rebind both CPUs, schedule, GPIO/IRQ
readers and LCD state. Complete suffix traces, CPU states, clocks and counts are
identical; original trailer reads and returns execute again. Eight fixed trace
corruptions reject. MCU NVM/knowledge/erase history, helper/tail, source/program,
download context and own callers remain exact outside native firmware patches.

Consumed logical pads are known in the successful paths. Unresolved unconsumed
voltage intervals still make the separate board-qualification check reject.
This is conditional first-stage execution, not physical wiring/clock validation,
complete SMF interleaving, the later external-controller programmer/recovery,
normal boot/MIDI/audio or any completed whole-instrument acceptance row.
All requirements remain in objective.txt/coverage.txt. No fuzzing or physical
traffic runs; manufacturer assets and detailed analysis stay local.
