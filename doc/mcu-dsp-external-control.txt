External WAI and DSP-reset events on dsp56300

mcu_dsp_fetch_external_wait and mcu_dsp_fetch_external_reset use the regular
McuDspRuntime and independent DSP core only. Both start with the genuine five
MCU reset-vector periods and retain DSP reset hold from common tick zero.
Authored MCU instructions and a DSP NOP ROM isolate event arbitration; these
are explicit reference-effect timing profiles, not calibrated silicon stages
or original-firmware instrument acceptance.

WAI uses 90-tick MCU periods and 45-tick DSP periods. Initial MCU reset ends
at450; two recovery clocks end at540. Actual LDS/ANDCC/WAI reaches1350 with
the literal nine-byte WAI frame. Each IRQ coordinate runs with peer-first and
DSP-first ordering, then restores its joint idle checkpoint twice:

  IRQ tick       Idle periods   Handler entry   RTI completion   DSP periods
  1350           0              1890            2610             46
  1351           1              1980            2700             48
  1395           1              1980            2700             48
  1439           1              1980            2700             48
  1440           1              1980            2700             48
  1441           2              2070            2790             50

The six-period IRQ entry reuses the actual WAI frame; eight-period RTI restores
the literal foreground PC, SP, flags and registers. The external IRQ deasserts
at2000. Every completed DSP period checks independent full registers, PCU and
retirement expectations. Simultaneous events check their declared order;
non-lattice edges occur without a fabricated DSP completion. All12 profiles
retain24 complete event/native/IRQ/RTI suffixes across fresh runtime owners.

The reset group instead uses 60-tick MCU periods. Its initial five vector
periods end at300. Actual LDAB/STAB releases DSP reset through PORTU at480;
two EXTAL recovery clocks finish at570. The next instruction boundary at540
still retains30 recovery ticks. Four MCU/external and DSP/peer order profiles
each restore twice, retaining8 complete GPIO/input/native suffixes.
At570, peer-first sees reset at569 with one tick remaining; DSP-first sees
completed recovery. Actual F1/F2 complete at615/660, with zero retirements and
one CLK2 edge. Full DSP registers retain the independent reset expectations.
No prepared CPU continuation or reset-relative epoch replaces these intervals.
