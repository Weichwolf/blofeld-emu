Native MCU/DSP reference scheduling

The regular Board uses McuDspRuntime with dsp56300. MCU operations supply actual
IPBus/reset/WAI periods; the runtime advances genuine DSP F1/F2, native effects,
PCU and retirement boundaries against their common timebase. Board wiring,
external event cursors and host transport remain caller-owned. These are
explicit reference-effect contracts, not calibrated physical pipeline clocks.

Shared writes require explicit before/after-MCU ordering. Only the actual bus
write observer delivers the signal change. Missing/wrong-address delivery,
unknown ties, missing cycle inputs, exhausted budgets and reentrant snapshot/
completion calls refuse. The native shared-write matrix checks real output
pads, full DSP registers/progress and literal MCU/DSP time; no assembler or
instruction-completion backend supplies the execution.

Delay-slot failures

Authored HCS12 NOP;LDX #ffff;DBNE X,self starts from its real reset vector.
Five reset-vector periods consume450 common ticks. DSP hold starts at zero;
release at450 and two actual recovery clocks finish at540. The MCU NOP/LDX
setup reaches720, eight bus periods,80 local ticks and16 oscillator periods.
One/two/three real DSP NOPs precede MOVE X0,Y:>$2000, with X0=0 from the declared
cold CPU state. Their genuine prefetch fills the long MOVE's extension before
its write. No prepared instruction, register injection or epoch shift places
that fault in a chosen MCU slot.

  Consumed DBNE slots   0     1     2
  MCU completed tick    720   810   900
  MCU offered tick      810   900   990
  DSP completed tick    720   810   900
  Attempted write tick  765   855   945
  DSP periods           4     6     8
  DSP retirements       1     2     3
  CLK2 edges            2     3     4

Every accepted period checks independent full native registers, PC and progress.
The real write retains value, address, instruction PC and its uncharged deadline;
DBNE's actual X decrement survives the interrupted operation. Both owners
quarantine, failed snapshots refuse and restoring the DSP alone cannot resume
the coordinator. Each case restores the joint cut twice, comparing complete
native effect/clock traces and the exact contextual failure. The native snapshot
restores both CPUs, frontend, RAM and clocks together; external observer cursors
remain explicit caller state.

The consolidated mcu_dsp_fetch_schedule group has13 cases, including these three
fault profiles/six restored suffixes and three pre-mutation clock/contract
refusals. It replaces the former mcu_dsp_schedule test; its shared-write cases
were already covered by the stronger native matrix and are not duplicated.
The separate mcu_dsp_runtime group also checks six causal write/input/DSP orders,
two cyclic refusals, fresh-owner checkpoints and contextual native input failure.

  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-mcu-dsp-fetch-schedule-test blofeld-mcu-dsp-runtime-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -j1 --output-on-failure -R '^(mcu_dsp_fetch_schedule|mcu_dsp_runtime)$'

Repeat with the separate thread preset. Scope is single-threaded emulated
ordering, not safe concurrent access or full Desktop/Keyboard acceptance.
Native WAI/reset controls are in mcu-dsp-wait.txt and mcu-dsp-cop-reset.txt;
external event arbitration is in dsp-fetch-events.txt. No allocating/throwing
diagnostic or snapshot facility belongs in a future real-time audio callback.

Legacy instruction-completion fixtures

McuDspSchedule and remaining instruction-completion fixtures still use the old
backend. Their independent observation/epoch contracts do not qualify the
regular runtime. The four unchanged original_scheduled_coupling cases remain
separate supporting evidence (scheduled-coupling.txt); they do not demonstrate
native replacement, complete stock boot or physical board timing. Full original
firmware operation, MIDI/audio/USB, persistence, updates/recovery and real-time
acceptance remain open (objective.txt and coverage.txt).
