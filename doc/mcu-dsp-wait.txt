Native MCU WAI/RTI with dsp56300

mcu_dsp_fetch_wait uses McuDspRuntime and the independent DSP core exclusively.
A real MCU reset vector establishes five IPBus periods (450 common ticks).
The DSP starts held in reset, then consumes two recovery clocks (90 ticks)
before its first F1. Native MOVE #>1,X1;CLR A;ADD X1,A;BRA *-1 executes through
actual F1/F2 and staged effects; no instruction or register state is injected.
This is an explicit effect-per-reference-cycle profile, not measured silicon
pipeline placement or physical MCU/DSP clock/reset qualification.

Fifteen cases retain the prior literal MCU expectations: nine WAI stack bytes,
512 OSC or2560 PHY idle periods to the1024-OSC-edge RTI at common tick47970,
TIM gating, six-period interrupt entry reusing the WAI frame, and original
foreground registers restored by the authored handler/RTI. Four OSC/PHY and
TIM profiles each restore an actual idle checkpoint twice (eight suffixes).
Every suffix checks independent native ADD results and native retirement PCs;
full DSP registers/flags, progress, pending effects, F1 addresses, F2 capture
and completion ticks are compared across the two restored continuations.

Existing, pre-observed and sampled input-capture IRQs cancel uncommitted idle
offers without clocking the DSP. A pin IRQ raised during an admitted DSP period
finishes the committed MCU period before wake selection. Wrong WAI context,
missing idle completion and an unqualified wait order refuse explicitly.
An empty zero-budget wait closes without any time; an unfinished actual WAI
instruction cannot masquerade as that empty idle boundary. Snapshot admission
requires the actual idle slice to be completed.
A real write to reserved Y:$2000 during WAI reports the operation,address,
MCU/DSP PCs and time; failed execution and new joint snapshots refuse, while
only genuinely completed MCU and DSP periods enter their respective ledgers.
Diagnostic chip snapshots retain the failed clock/state and cannot resume
native execution even in a fresh owner. PHY-derived MCU horizons may fall
between45-tick DSP reference boundaries; that residue remains pending rather
than changing either emulated clock.
