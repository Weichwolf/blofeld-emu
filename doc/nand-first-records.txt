Original runtime records from the prepared first NAND page

  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-original-nand-records-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -j1 -R '^original_nand_records$' --output-on-failure

The optional wrapper verifies the official 1.25 ZIP, independently decodes its
main transfer and checks the first runtime page against the size/hash/source
offset in nand-first-records-reference.json. Original bytes stay in ignored
Testing/original-nand-records.trace.page.bin; they are not build/source assets.
This is an updater-source reference, not a verified physical NAND image.

An own native caller supplies the supported BIU/GPIO/clock conditions and one
explicitly ready candidate page. The unchanged original packing loop reads all
528 bytes through the timed NAND input. An own native jump joins its returned
first word to the original post-read continuation. A separate FM13-37 short-BSR
encoding check establishes that continuation. No host PC/frame/register edits,
replacement callees or host operand-RAM payload writes supply the path. Original
reset/clear/initial refill setup remains outside this fixture.

The actual page contains 14 complete records and four of eight payload words in
the fifteenth. An independent raw-word oracle checks all 131 executed stores:
29 P words, 98 X words and four Y words. Fifteen actual original MOVEM writes
patch the next store from the selected original template. The subsequent stores
must use the independent expected bank/address/value, including the transition
from a previously cached X-store instruction to Y. X/Y and tail guards retain
their supplied values; untouched adjacent P RAM remains explicitly unknown.

A joint checkpoint immediately after the first Y-template patch retains native
machine/clock, modified code, NAND state, output timing and verification cursors.
A fresh identically contracted owner and fresh source callbacks reproduce every
recorded store/template/state/time row, the final memory and the complete debug
state. These are trusted in-process snapshots, not a portable board-state format.

After consuming the supplied page, the original loader begins its next refill.
The prepared-page fixture rejects the first asserted write-enable with bus
address/PC/OMR/page/column context. It does not silently ignore a command, provide
a second page or fabricate completion. At that boundary the final record still
has four words left, its real loop/call frames remain live, and post-page NAND
readiness is unknown. The two older complete read/packing traces stay unchanged.

The nominal timing contract and limits are in nand-read-timing.txt. Actual BIU
latency, default/reset behavior, transient Port F modes, reserved writes, stock
mask ROM and remaining contents require separate evidence. Full original refill,
scheduled Desktop/Keyboard boot, normal MIDI/audio, updates/recovery and physical
comparison remain open in coverage.txt. Detailed original data and traces remain
local; only source locations, sizes, hashes and test expectations are published.
