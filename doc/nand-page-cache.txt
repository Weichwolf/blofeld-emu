NAND selected-page cache

NandArray retains a positive page pointer across sequential reads. Map insertion,
rebalancing and Page replacement preserve its address. Missing pages stay unknown
and uncached. Copy/move assignment clears transient pointers before map replacement;
move also clears the source. Copies begin with an empty cache. Cached reads require
one model owner. Image serialization excludes the cache; rebuild all NAND owners
after this class-layout change.
