Reference Board pipeline

The regular Board uses McuDspRuntime and dsp56300. Config::power_on optionally
starts the actual MCU digital POR at zero. Supply synchronization, RESET pad,
DSP mode/PINIT/recovery, clock and ordering contracts explicitly. External IRQ/
DMA requests stay unknown; supported internal DAX/ESAI requests derive from
real peripheral state. These are declared experimental profiles.

The DSP starts held in reset at zero. Initial DSP pin observations apply at
the native attachment boundary: zero during MCU POR, or the consumed reset-
vector time when attaching without POR. They precede a configured timed input at that same
boundary. Genuine timed inputs before the boundary refuse; their timestamps
are never shifted. Held MCU registers are unqualified until vector execution.
Status counts actual DSP pipeline periods and architectural retirements
separately. Restore prepares
fresh native owners, then publishes owners/control together. MIDI queues and
partial bits rewind; external PCM bindings retire and USB revisions advance.

reference_checkpoint covers both zero/450 attachment profiles with literal
clock/CPU/reset endpoints, exact-boundary ESAI input ordering, four restored
next-edge continuations and refusal of a genuinely earlier timed input.

  cmake --build --preset strict --target blofeld-reference-checkpoint-test
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -j1 --output-on-failure -R '^reference_checkpoint$'

Repeat with the separate thread preset.

reference_pipeline checks literal POR/vector/NOP timing, held/recovering/run
forks, strict missing inputs, MIDI partial-bit replay and PCM/USB lifecycle.
original_board_power_on pins unchanged firmware and the independent MCU
bootstrap ledger through Board POR, with explicit laboratory inputs and DSP
held without ROM. These checks do not establish full stock boot, original DSP
audio, USB-MIDI, physical reset timing, real-time operation or hardware equivalence.
