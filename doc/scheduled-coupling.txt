Legacy nominal scheduling fixture

This document describes the remaining legacy test API, not the regular runtime.
Current independent-core composition is in own-dsp-integration.txt;
McuDspRuntime is the connected MCU/DSP runtime. The legacy dependency and its
reference patches still exist for tests pending migration.

DspMachine::plan_step() previews native nominal duration without executing an
instruction, consuming a peripheral operand or advancing the clock. Latched REP
and self-modifying MOVEM tests account from the executed word. Literal primary
instruction costs provide independent expectations.

DspScheduledRun completes instructions within an absolute horizon at its declared
reference_completion_boundary. Before/through selects exclusive/inclusive ties;
it does not reconstruct physical pipeline or bus phases. McuDspSchedule owns
common-time accounting and shared-write ordering (mcu-dsp-schedule.txt).

Unknown code, changed pending plans, reversed time, callback mutation, budget
exhaustion and unilateral restore reject visibly. A failed instruction can have
partial effects; it is not automatic rollback. A trusted joint restore requires
native machine, clock and schedule ownership together. Checkpoints and callbacks
are single-threaded reference facilities, outside any realtime audio callback.
See dsp-checkpoints.txt and dsp-run-clock.txt for the related component contracts.

The optional original fixture executes a bounded SPI bootstrap prefix with
explicit12MHz MCU/DSP inputs, two reset phases and both exact-tie orders. It checks
97 frames,94 stage words and the original RAM-clear continuation. This historical
map intentionally rejects the reserved Y:002000 write and replays that failure
from a healthy joint checkpoint. That failure is a legacy fixture expectation,
not the current independent-core runtime frontier or a physical-chip observation.
It does not establish full startup, live stores, voices, MIDI/audio or recovery.
Current connected evidence and all remaining requirements are in coverage.txt.
